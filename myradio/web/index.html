            FROM FIRST SOUND TO FREQUENCY

            A station in a few steps.

            No AI account or broadcast host needed to start privately.

            01 / Bring your sound

            Import audio you own or have permission to play. Music joins rotation; speech and ads play only when queued or scheduled.

            02 / Make it flow

            Queue favorites from Library, then return to Studio. Start station runs local playout. Listen here opens your browser monitor. Stop ends playout; it is not pause.

            03 / Find your rhythm

            Schedule one-off or recurring moments. Events join after the current track, not at an exact interruption point. Missed events are not replayed in a flood.

            04 / Work with your operator

            Try “status” or “help” in chat. Without an AI connection this is a command assistant. Connect your chosen provider for open-ended requests, and approve each proposed change. This is not an autonomous background DJ.

            05 / Share deliberately

            Configure your Icecast host in Broadcast. Review the destination and your rights before Go live. Hosting is separate; no host is included. Bytes sent do not prove public listener reception.

            Your studio stays yours

            Audio stays local. An optional AI provider receives chat and station metadata, with consent. Lock removes browser control credentials, but does not stop the station. Keep your launch code private.

            Back up and restore with the documented myradio-backup command. Portable backups exclude credentials. Songs and the DJ voice are made on this computer once their makers are set up; your connected agent can write the DJ’s words.
