- Three hour-long on-air receipts on 2026-11-06 (see the three *-hour.md files): Mac mini (Apple silicon), RTX 4080 desktop

- Studio: one page, a mixing-desk "Console"; Dial, a pixel-art radio mascot with moods; levels, trophies and badges from
  real station facts; a "Fade to next" button; separate crates for music and DJ clips; approval cards before anything

- First run (being finished for launch): download the models, link up, choose a station style, choose the DJ, first songs on air.
  `myradio setup` (one command that detects Mac / NVIDIA / DGX Spark and downloads everything) is planned for launch day and
  NOT finished yet: write its copy, but mark every sentence that depends on it with [NEEDS: myradio setup].
- Connect your agent: users hook up their favourite agent (Claude Code, Codex, any MCP agent). The agent is the DJ's brain:
  it writes what the DJ says and programs the station; myradio's voice speaks it. myradio itself does not read Twitch chat or
  screenshots; the user's agent does that and asks the station for songs.
- DJ voices, the user's recommendations: local Qwen3-TTS CustomVoice (top local choice; "Jessica" has read the news on the
  user's live station for weeks), Drama 3 (prompted performance, big machines), IndexTTS2; cloud: ElevenLabs, announcer voice
  "Guy". Chatterbox is a fast, decent MIT option.
- Real examples (lead with these): Frank's live Dota stream: the agent grabbed screenshots of the stream, sent them to the DJ,
  and the station made songs as he played ("Riki with a Dagon", "Boots of Travel", "They Can't Doubt the Hands"). Others the
  user wants shown: your chat sung live on Twitch; work and study notes turned into songs; a family party with special
  dedications and memories. Tagline: "Everyone gets their own bard." "Made for humans to enjoy new media and a life of open
  ended possibilities." Thanks to YuE2 for the model powering it.
